# Evidence-First S&B Interview

**Role-agnostic interview preparation / 跨岗位情景与行为面试准备**

A reusable Agent Skill for interviewing in **any company or role**. It complements the existing [Evidence-First Resume](../../SKILL.md) skill without replacing or changing it.

This skill supports both:
- **B — Behavioral:** Describe something you actually did, with credible evidence, a spoken STAR structure, and challenging follow-ups.
- **S — Situational:** Explain what you would do in a hypothetical situation using objectives, judgment, trade-offs, stakeholder communication, action, contingencies and verification.

**Core philosophy:** Verified evidence → relevant story or judgment framework → natural answer → realistic follow-ups → mock interview → improvement.

**核心原则：** 先核对事实，再选择故事或情景决策框架，写成自然口述答案，通过追问和模拟面试持续打磨。绝不把提案当成已上线的成果，也不为凑故事而编造冲突和业绩。

## How to use / 如何使用

1. Add the canonical `SKILL.md` to your agent/assistant skill instructions, or paste its content into the working conversation.
2. Supply a resume and relevant source material (project reports, notes, logs, outcome evidence) as needed.
3. Optionally provide the target role description and company principles. These are **adaptation inputs**, not mandatory defaults.
4. Request one of the modes: **AUDIT, BANK, MAP, DRAFT-B, DRAFT-S, DEEP-DIVE, MOCK, REVISE, SERIAL, HANDOFF**.
5. Use the private template outside this public repository to store completed personal interview stories and evidence.

### 中文范例：按我们之前的逐题流程准备

> 请使用 Evidence-First S&B Interview Skill，先审阅我的简历和项目证据，建立可以跨公司复用的故事库。然后逐题训练 Behavioral Interview：每题给中英逐段对照的 90–120 秒 STAR 正式回答、3 个重要追问、8 个高强度追问、面试官评价与事实边界、记忆主线。我说“继续”后才开始下一题。不要默认 Amazon Leadership Principles。

### English example: full coaching

> Use Evidence-First S&B Interview for a Product Manager role. Audit my actual experience, map it to the JD, then prepare each story with a 2-minute STAR, three high-priority probes, eight deeper follow-ups and a credibility assessment. Move to the next story only when I say continue.

### Situational mock interview / 情景模拟

> 请作为咨询岗位面试官，每次给我一个假设场景（如客户优先级冲突、资源不足、伦理判断）。先听我的回答，再逐步追问和评价。不要把假设故事写成我的真实工作经历，也不要提前剧透标准答案。

> Act as a bank graduate interview assessor. Ask me one ethics or risk scenario at a time, evaluate my answer and press on trade-offs. Do not reveal the ideal response until I've answered.

### Cross-company story reuse / 跨公司故事复用

> Use my verified story bank to prepare for a customer-facing engineering interview. Match each story to the new job requirements; don't assume the new employer uses Amazon's rubric or re-invent the underlying experience.

## Relationship to the resume skill

The resume workflow turns work into **short, verifiable accomplishment bullets**. The S&B workflow uses those bullets as **leads**, inspects source evidence, and develops defensible spoken stories and situational reasoning. A resume bullet is not sufficient proof of stakeholder reactions, exact dialogue, implementation depth or verified results.

Both use the same strict implementation-status language: **designed / implemented / locally tested / deployed / adopted / measured / proposed**.

## Privacy

This repository contains **generic instructions and blank templates only**, not completed personal interview answers or confidential employer case details. Keep private story banks and proprietary source records outside a public repository unless you explicitly choose to publish them.
